# Introduction to Investing

**Source:** [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) Investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing)  
**Reviewed:** October 30, 2026  
**Audience:** People learning basic investing concepts

## What investing means

Investing means putting money into assets such as stocks or bonds with the hope of earning a return over time. A return may come from an asset increasing in value or from interest or dividend payments. Investments carry risk, and their value can fluctuate.

## Compound growth and time

Compound growth occurs when an investment earns returns and those returns also earn returns. Keeping investments invested over many years and contributing regularly can give compounding more time to work. Starting earlier can increase that time, though investment returns are not guaranteed.

## Managing risk

Investments can lose value. An investor's time horizon—the time available to reach a goal—and comfort with risk are factors to consider when choosing investment products.

## Asset allocation and diversification

Asset allocation means dividing investments among asset classes, such as stocks, bonds, and cash. Diversification means spreading investments across different assets rather than relying on one investment. These approaches may help manage risk, but they do not prevent every loss.
