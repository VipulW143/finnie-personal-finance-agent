# Diversify Your Investments

Source: [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) Investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/save-and-invest/diversify-your-investments)
Reviewed November 5, 2026.
Capture: selected educational summary; not the full source article.

Diversification means spreading money among different investments instead of relying on a single investment. Gains in some holdings may offset losses in others. The aim is to reduce the effect of one investment performing poorly on the overall portfolio.

Diversification cannot guarantee that a portfolio will avoid losses when markets fall. It is a way to reduce concentration, not a guarantee of profit or a substitute for understanding the assets held. The number of holdings alone does not explain whether their risks are different.
