# Roth IRA contributions and distributions

Source: Internal Revenue Service (IRS), Roth IRAs
https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/roth-iras
Reviewed November 4, 2026.

Contributions to a Roth IRA cannot be deducted from taxable income. Qualified distributions from a Roth IRA are tax-free when the requirements are satisfied.

The same combined IRA contribution limit applies to Roth and traditional IRAs. Roth IRA contributions may be limited by filing status and income. This source does not determine a person's eligibility or supply tax-year-specific limits.

A Roth IRA is distinct from a designated Roth account in an employer retirement plan. Refer to the IRS's linked comparison for the detailed differences.
