# Credit Reports vs. Credit Scores

Source: [Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB)](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-the-difference-between-a-credit-report-and-a-credit-score-en-2069/)
Reviewed November 5, 2026.
Capture: selected educational summary; not the full source article.

A credit report records credit activity, payment history, and the status of credit accounts. A credit score is calculated using information from a report. They are related but are not the same thing.

A person can have multiple scores because reporting-company information, scoring models, loan products, and calculation dates differ. Reports and scores can influence access to credit and the interest rate offered. Errors in a report can artificially reduce a score, so checking and correcting the underlying information is important before applying for a loan. A score is a model's summary, not the complete credit history.
