# Researching Investments

Source: [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) Investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/getting-started/researching-investments)
Reviewed November 5, 2026.
Capture: selected educational summary; not the full source article.

Research is part of investment due diligence. Companies offering stocks or bonds to the public must provide specified information at the offering stage, and companies and bond issuers provide periodic disclosures. Those materials help investors evaluate the security and issuer.

A lack of SEC registration can be a warning sign, since investment scams often involve unregistered companies. Research involves examining disclosures rather than relying only on a promoter's claims. Registration and available information support investigation; they should not be interpreted as a promise that an investment cannot lose value.
