# Treasury Notes

Source: [U.S. Department of the Treasury](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/marketable-securities/treasury-notes/)
Reviewed November 5, 2026.
Capture: selected educational summary; not the full source article.

Treasury notes are U.S. Treasury marketable securities issued with terms of two, three, five, seven, or ten years. They pay interest every six months at a fixed rate until maturity.

A note's original term distinguishes it from a short-term Treasury bill and from a Treasury bond with a longer original maturity. The fixed coupon describes the interest payment terms, rather than the price a seller may receive in the market before maturity. TreasuryDirect provides auction and pricing information separately from the general product description.
