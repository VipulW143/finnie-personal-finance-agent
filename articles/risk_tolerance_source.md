# Gauge Your Risk Tolerance

Source: [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) Investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/save-and-invest/gauge-your-risk-tolerance)
Reviewed November 5, 2026.
Capture: selected educational summary; not the full source article.

Risk tolerance concerns how much investment uncertainty and potential loss an investor can accept. Time horizon also matters: money needed soon has less time to recover from a decline. Selling an investment during a downturn can lock in a loss when the money is needed for a short-term goal.

For longer-term goals, growth investments may help address inflation, while savings products can have lower growth after inflation and taxes. This trade-off does not mean that taking more risk guarantees greater returns. A suitable level of risk depends on the goal and the investor's circumstances.
