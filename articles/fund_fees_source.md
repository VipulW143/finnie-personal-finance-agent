# Mutual Fund and ETF Fees and Expenses

Source: [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) Investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/glossary/mutual-fund-fees-and-expenses)
Reviewed November 4, 2026.

Mutual funds and ETFs incur transaction costs and recurring operating expenses. Operating costs can include investment advisory, marketing, distribution, brokerage, custody, and accounting expenses.

Funds pass costs to shareholders through fees and expenses. A standardized fee table near the front of a fund's prospectus identifies these costs.

Costs vary among funds. A higher-cost fund must perform better to deliver the same return as a lower-cost fund. Even small fee differences can have a substantial effect on returns over time.
