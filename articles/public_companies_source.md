# Public Companies

Source: [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) Investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/how-stock-markets-work/public-companies)
Reviewed November 5, 2026.
Capture: selected educational summary; not the full source article.

Public companies disclose information that investors can use to research their businesses and finances. An annual Form 10-K includes audited financial statements. Form 10-Q provides quarterly information, including unaudited statements, for the first three fiscal quarters.

Form 8-K reports specified material events between periodic reports. Proxy materials supply information relevant to shareholder voting. These documents serve different purposes, so reading only a stock price or company headline does not replace examining filings. The reporting schedule and document type help distinguish a periodic financial update from an event-driven disclosure.
