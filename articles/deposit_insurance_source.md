# Understanding Deposit Insurance

Source: [Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC)](https://www.fdic.gov/resources/deposit-insurance/understanding-deposit-insurance)
Reviewed November 5, 2026.
Capture: selected educational summary; not the full source article.

FDIC deposit insurance protects eligible deposits at an FDIC-insured bank when the bank fails. The standard coverage limit is $250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, for each account ownership category. It is not a separate $250,000 limit for every account a person opens at the same bank.

Coverage depends on the bank, depositor, and legal ownership category. Multiple accounts in the same category at one bank are considered together under the applicable rules. Before assuming that a balance is fully insured, identify the institution and ownership category and use the FDIC's current guidance or insurance estimator.
