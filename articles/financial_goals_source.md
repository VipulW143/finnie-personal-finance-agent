# Define Your Goals

Source: [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) Investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/save-and-invest/define-your-goals)
Reviewed November 5, 2026.
Capture: selected educational summary; not the full source article.

A financial plan begins by identifying what the saver wants to achieve. List important goals in priority order and determine how many years are available to meet each one. The time available helps identify saving or investment options that fit the goal.

The SEC emphasizes learning basic principles, forming a plan, and following it consistently. Goal planning connects the desired outcome with a time frame and the amount that must be saved. Different goals can have different deadlines, so a single time horizon should not automatically be applied to every part of a person's finances.
