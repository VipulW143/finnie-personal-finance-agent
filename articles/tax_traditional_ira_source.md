# Traditional IRA contributions and distributions

Source: Internal Revenue Service (IRS), Traditional IRAs
https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/traditional-iras
Reviewed November 4, 2026.

A traditional IRA is an account for retirement saving with tax advantages.

Contributions to a traditional IRA may be fully or partially deductible depending on filing status and income. A contribution is not automatically fully deductible.

Amounts in a traditional IRA, including earnings and gains, generally are not taxed until a distribution or withdrawal. The IRS directs taxpayers to Publications 590-A and 590-B for detailed contribution and distribution rules.

This article explains general federal tax treatment. It does not determine a person's deduction eligibility or supply tax-year-specific thresholds.
