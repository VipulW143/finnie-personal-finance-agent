# Series I Savings Bonds

Source: [U.S. Department of the Treasury](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/savings-bonds/i-bonds/)
Reviewed November 5, 2026.
Capture: selected educational summary; not the full source article.

A Series I savings bond earns a combined rate based on a fixed component and an inflation component. The fixed component stays constant for that bond, while the inflation component resets every six months. Interest accrues monthly and compounds semiannually.

I bonds can earn interest for 30 years unless redeemed earlier. They generally cannot be redeemed during the first 12 months. Redemption before five years forfeits the last three months of interest. Unlike marketable Treasury securities, savings bonds are redeemed rather than traded on an exchange. Current rates and purchase limits should be checked on TreasuryDirect rather than assumed from a saved summary.
