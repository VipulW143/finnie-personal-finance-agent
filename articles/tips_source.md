# Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities (TIPS)

Source: [U.S. Department of the Treasury](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/marketable-securities/tips/)
Reviewed November 5, 2026.
Capture: selected educational summary; not the full source article.

Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities adjust principal with inflation and deflation using a Consumer Price Index measure. They are issued with terms of five, ten, or 30 years. Their interest rate is fixed, but interest payments vary because the rate is applied to the adjusted principal every six months.

At maturity, Treasury pays the inflation-adjusted principal or the original principal, whichever is greater. This maturity protection should not be confused with a guaranteed resale price before maturity. A holder can retain TIPS until maturity or sell them earlier. TIPS differ from Series I savings bonds in structure and tradability.
