# Stocks

Source: [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) Investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/investment-products/stocks)
Reviewed November 5, 2026.
Capture: selected educational summary; not the full source article.

Stocks represent ownership in a company. Investors may receive returns through a rising share price or dividend payments, but neither is assured. Common stock usually includes voting rights. Preferred stock generally lacks voting rights but has priority over common stock for dividends and claims on remaining assets in liquidation.

Stock prices can fall, and a company can fail. Owning shares is different from lending money to an issuer. Investors consider the company's business, financial condition, and the risks of the investment rather than assuming that a well-known company guarantees a positive return.
