# Disputing Credit Report Errors

Source: [Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB)](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/how-do-i-dispute-an-error-on-my-credit-report-en-314/)
Reviewed November 5, 2026.
Capture: selected educational summary; not the full source article.

A consumer can dispute inaccurate credit-report information with both the credit reporting company and the business that supplied the information. A written dispute identifies the account or item, explains the error and requested correction, and includes copies of supporting records.

Keep copies of letters and evidence. The credit reporting company investigates and forwards relevant information to the furnisher. If information is wrong or cannot be verified, the furnisher must update or remove it and notify the reporting companies. If an unresolved dispute remains, a consumer can request a statement in the report. Suspected identity theft has a separate recovery process through IdentityTheft.gov.
