# What Is Risk?

Source: [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) Investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/what-risk)
Reviewed November 5, 2026.
Capture: selected educational summary; not the full source article.

Investment risk includes the possibility of losing money or receiving less than expected. Different investments expose investors to different combinations of risk. Interest-rate changes can affect bond prices, and lack of liquidity can make an asset difficult to sell when needed.

Bank deposit insurance is different from protection for securities. FDIC insurance covers eligible bank deposits under its rules; it does not insure stocks or mutual funds against investment losses. Likewise, SIPC protection does not reimburse ordinary market losses. Evaluating an investment requires understanding what risks remain, even when an account or institution has a form of protection.
