# Target Date Funds — Investor Bulletin

Source: [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) Investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins/target-date-funds-investor-bulletin)
Reviewed November 5, 2026.
Capture: selected educational summary; not the full source article.

Target-date funds hold a mix of investments and change their asset allocation over time. Many invest through other funds, combining exposure to stocks, bonds, and other assets. A target year often corresponds to an anticipated retirement year.

The investment mix generally becomes more conservative as the target date approaches, often shifting toward bonds. The fund's adviser manages that changing allocation. The target date is part of an investment strategy, rather than a guarantee that an investor will have a particular amount at retirement. Understanding the actual allocation and strategy matters when evaluating a target-date fund.
