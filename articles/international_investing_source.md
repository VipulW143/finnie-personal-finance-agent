# International Investing

Source: [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) Investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/investment-products/international-investing)
Reviewed November 5, 2026.
Capture: selected educational summary; not the full source article.

Investing outside the United States can provide exposure to other economies and help diversify a portfolio. However, international investments introduce risks beyond those of a comparable domestic investment.

Changes in currency exchange rates can increase or decrease returns measured in U.S. dollars. Foreign markets can have different trading costs, accounting standards, disclosures, and legal protections. Political or social developments can affect investments, and some markets have less liquidity. Access to information may be more difficult because of language or reporting differences. Diversification across countries does not remove these risks or guarantee a positive return.
