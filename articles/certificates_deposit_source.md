# Certificates of Deposit (CDs)

Source: [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) Investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/investment-products/certificates-deposit-cds)
Reviewed November 5, 2026.
Capture: selected educational summary; not the full source article.

A certificate of deposit is a deposit held for an agreed period in exchange for interest. At maturity the depositor receives the money deposited and the interest provided under the account terms. CDs differ in term, rate structure, and conditions.

Before opening a CD, read the disclosures to determine whether the interest rate is fixed or variable, when interest is paid, and whether early withdrawal causes a penalty. Inflation can reduce the purchasing power of the money returned. A CD's deposit protections and withdrawal rules should not be confused with the risks or liquidity of an investment fund.
