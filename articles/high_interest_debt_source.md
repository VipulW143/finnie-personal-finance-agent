# Pay Credit Cards or Other High Interest Debt

Source: [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) Investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/save-and-invest/pay-credit-cards-or-other-high-interest)
Reviewed November 5, 2026.
Capture: selected educational summary; not the full source article.

Paying down high-interest debt reduces the interest charged on that debt. The SEC contrasts that cost reduction with uncertain investment returns. A promised investment return should not be treated as a certain way to outpace costly borrowing.

For multiple credit-card balances, the page describes directing extra payments toward the highest-interest balance while continuing required minimum payments on the others. Once that balance is paid off, extra payments can move to the next highest rate. This method focuses on reducing interest expense rather than selecting accounts only by their outstanding balance.
