# How Credit Card Interest Is Calculated

Source: [Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB)](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/how-does-my-credit-card-company-calculate-the-amount-of-interest-i-owe-en-51/)
Reviewed November 5, 2026.
Capture: selected educational summary; not the full source article.

Many card issuers calculate interest using daily balances or an average daily balance. Different transaction types can carry different rates, including purchases, cash advances, and other uses of the account. The APR for one balance should not automatically be applied to every transaction.

If a purchase grace period applies, paying the balance in full by the due date can avoid purchase interest. When interest accrues daily, paying earlier can reduce the balance on which interest is calculated. Amounts paid above the minimum generally go first to the highest-rate balance, while the issuer generally chooses how to allocate the minimum payment.
