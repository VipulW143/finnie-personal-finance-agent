# Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs)

Source: [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) Investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/investment-products/real-estate-investment-trusts-reits)
Reviewed November 5, 2026.
Capture: selected educational summary; not the full source article.

A real estate investment trust owns or operates income-producing real estate or related assets, such as commercial properties or mortgages. REITs allow investors to participate in real-estate income without directly purchasing and managing a building.

Publicly traded REITs trade on an exchange. Public non-traded REITs are registered but do not trade on an exchange and can be difficult to sell. Their values may also be less visible. A non-traded REIT may fund distributions from offering proceeds or borrowing, so a cash distribution should not automatically be interpreted as operating profit. Liquidity and distribution sources are important differences to examine.
