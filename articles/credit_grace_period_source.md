# Credit Card Grace Periods

Source: [Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB)](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-grace-period-for-a-credit-card-en-47/)
Reviewed November 5, 2026.
Capture: selected educational summary; not the full source article.

A grace period runs between the close of a billing cycle and the payment due date. When a card offers a purchase grace period and the conditions are met, paying the balance in full by the due date can avoid interest on purchases. Issuers are not required to offer one.

Carrying a balance can cause loss of the grace period. Interest may then accrue on the unpaid balance and on new purchases from their transaction dates. Grace periods generally apply to purchases, not cash advances, which typically accrue interest immediately. Paying only the minimum is different from paying the balance in full.
