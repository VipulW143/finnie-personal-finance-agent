# Understanding Fees

Source: [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) Investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/getting-started/understanding-fees)
Reviewed November 5, 2026.
Capture: selected educational summary; not the full source article.

Investment products and services have costs that can significantly reduce a portfolio's value over time. Even small differences in recurring fees can accumulate over many years. The SEC illustrates this with hypothetical portfolios using different annual fee rates; those illustrations are not promised investment outcomes.

Questions to ask include the total cost to buy and sell, ongoing account charges, how much growth is needed to break even, and whether similar products have different costs. Ask how an investment professional is paid, such as by commission, transaction charge, flat fee, or assets managed. Product costs and account-service costs should both be considered.
