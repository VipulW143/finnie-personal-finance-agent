# Five Questions to Ask Before You Invest

Source: [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) Investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/getting-started/five-questions-ask-you-invest)
Reviewed November 5, 2026.
Capture: selected educational summary; not the full source article.

Before investing, investigate whether the seller is licensed, whether the securities offering is registered or qualifies for an exemption, how potential returns compare with risks, whether the investment is understood, and where independent help is available.

Read a prospectus or disclosure statement and ask questions about how the investment makes money. Greater potential return comes with greater risk, including greater potential losses. Registration provides access to important information about a company, but does not eliminate risk. Checking a promoter's background is useful before accepting a sales pitch or sending money.
