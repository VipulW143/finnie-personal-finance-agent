# Annuities

Source: [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) Investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/investment-products/annuities)
Reviewed November 5, 2026.
Capture: selected educational summary; not the full source article.

An annuity is a contract with an insurance company. The purchaser makes a lump-sum payment or a series of payments, and the insurer agrees to make periodic payments immediately or at a future date. Annuities are generally designed for long-term goals, such as retirement income.

Costs, risks, and payment terms differ by contract. Early withdrawals can trigger surrender charges and may have tax consequences or penalties. Payment promises depend on the insurer's ability to meet its obligations. An immediate annuity begins payments relatively soon, while a deferred annuity starts payments later. Reading the specific contract is essential.
