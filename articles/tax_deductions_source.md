# Standard deduction versus itemized deductions

Both the standard deduction and itemized deductions reduce federal taxable income. Eligible individuals generally choose one method.

- The standard deduction is a set amount based on filing status. The IRS adjusts it annually for inflation.
- Itemizing means listing eligible expenses on Schedule A (Form 1040) and totaling the amounts the rules allow. Examples include qualifying charitable gifts, mortgage interest, and certain medical expenses. Limits can apply.
- The two methods differ in how the deduction is determined: a set amount for the standard deduction or a total of eligible expenses for itemized deductions.
