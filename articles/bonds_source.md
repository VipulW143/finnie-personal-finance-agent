# Bonds

Source: [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) Investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/investment-products/bonds-or-fixed-income-products/bonds)
Reviewed November 5, 2026.
Capture: selected educational summary; not the full source article.

A bond is a loan made by an investor to a government, municipality, corporation, or other issuer. The issuer promises interest and repayment of the face value at maturity under the bond's terms. A bondholder is a creditor, rather than an owner like a stockholder.

Credit risk is the possibility that the issuer cannot make the promised payments. Interest-rate risk means that existing bond prices generally fall when market interest rates rise. Inflation can reduce the purchasing power of payments, and liquidity risk can make selling difficult. Some bonds can be called, meaning the issuer repays them before maturity according to the terms.
