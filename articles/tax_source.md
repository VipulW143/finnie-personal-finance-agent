# U.S. Federal Individual Tax Basics

**Source:** [Internal Revenue Service (IRS) - Credits and deductions](https://www.irs.gov/credits-and-deductions)  
**Reviewed:** November 3, 2026  
**Audience:** People learning general U.S. federal individual income-tax concepts

## Tax credits and deductions

A tax deduction reduces taxable income. A tax credit reduces the amount of tax due. The IRS explains that credits and deductions can help lower a tax bill or increase a refund. Which credits or deductions may apply depends on the specific rules and a person's circumstances.

## Boundaries

This source covers general U.S. federal tax concepts. It does not cover state, local, territorial, or non-U.S. tax law, and it does not determine an individual's eligibility, tax liability, or refund.

## Source note

This summary paraphrases the linked IRS page. It intentionally avoids copying year-specific thresholds or amounts so the source remains a basic learning resource; current rules should be checked with the IRS for the relevant tax year.
