# Filing a U.S. Federal Individual Income-Tax Return

**Source:** [Internal Revenue Service (IRS) - How to file your taxes: step by step](https://www.irs.gov/how-to-file-your-taxes-step-by-step)  
**Reviewed:** November 3, 2026  
**Audience:** People learning the basic federal income-tax filing process

## General filing sequence

The IRS describes these general steps for filing an individual federal income-tax return: check whether a return must be filed, gather tax documents, review credits and deductions, file the return, receive any refund, and pay taxes owed on time. This is an overview, not a determination that any particular person must file.

## Filing extensions and payment

The IRS says an extension may provide more time to file, but it does not extend the time to pay tax owed. Deadlines and filing requirements can depend on the tax year and a person's circumstances. Check the IRS page for current deadlines and official instructions.

## Boundaries

This source covers general U.S. federal filing information. It does not cover state, local, territorial, or non-U.S. tax law, and it does not determine an individual's filing obligation, eligibility, tax liability, or refund.

## Source note

This summary paraphrases the linked IRS page. It avoids stating a specific tax-year deadline because filing deadlines can change and may vary in special circumstances.
