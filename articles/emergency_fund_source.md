# An essential guide to building an emergency fund

Source: [Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB)](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/an-essential-guide-to-building-an-emergency-fund/)
Reviewed November 4, 2026.

An emergency fund is a cash reserve for unplanned expenses or financial emergencies, such as repairs, medical bills, or lost income. It is separate from routine monthly spending.

The amount needed depends on the person's circumstances and past unexpected expenses. Having a reserve can reduce reliance on credit or loans after a financial shock.

Ways to build a reserve include setting a savings goal, making consistent contributions, monitoring progress, and saving some one-time income. Automatic transfers can support consistent saving, but account balances need monitoring to avoid overdrafts. The fund should be safe and accessible.
