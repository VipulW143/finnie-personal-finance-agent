# Treasury Bonds

Source: [U.S. Department of the Treasury](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/marketable-securities/treasury-bonds/)
Reviewed November 5, 2026.
Capture: selected educational summary; not the full source article.

Treasury bonds are U.S. Treasury marketable securities issued with terms of 20 or 30 years. They pay a fixed interest rate every six months until maturity. The rate is set at auction and remains fixed for the bond's life.

A holder may keep the bond until maturity or sell it before then. Treasury bonds are different from Series EE or Series I savings bonds. Interest is subject to federal income tax but not state or local income tax. Product descriptions and auction results should be distinguished from a current secondary-market quote.
