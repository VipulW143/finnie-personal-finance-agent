# Federal income tax treatment of 401(k) contributions

Employee contributions to a 401(k) can provide a federal income tax benefit. The timing depends on whether the employee makes traditional pretax contributions or designated Roth contributions.

- Traditional pretax 401(k) contributions generally reduce federal taxable income in the year of contribution. Withdrawals of pretax contributions and their earnings are generally taxable.
- Designated Roth 401(k) contributions are included in taxable income in the year of contribution. Qualified withdrawals, including earnings, are generally free of federal income tax.
