# Municipal Bonds

Source: [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) Investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/investment-products/bonds-or-fixed-income-products-0)
Reviewed November 5, 2026.
Capture: selected educational summary; not the full source article.

States, cities, counties, and other public entities issue municipal bonds to fund public projects or operations. General obligation bonds are backed by the issuer's taxing authority, while revenue bonds are backed by revenues associated with a specified project or source.

Interest on many municipal bonds is exempt from federal income tax and may qualify for state tax treatment depending on the bond and investor. Tax exemption is not universal and does not remove investment risk. The Municipal Securities Rulemaking Board's EMMA website provides disclosures, pricing, and related information that investors can use to research an issue.
