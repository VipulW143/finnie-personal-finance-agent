# Index Funds

Source: [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) Investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/investment-products/mutual-funds-and-exchange-traded-4)
Reviewed November 4, 2026.

An index fund is a mutual fund or ETF that seeks to track a market index. A market index measures a basket of securities. Investors cannot invest directly in an index.

Some funds hold all the securities in their index; others hold a sample. Index funds generally follow a passive strategy. They may have lower costs, but not every index fund is cheaper than an actively managed fund.

An index fund can lose value. Fees, trading costs, and tracking error may cause it to underperform its index. Tracking error means that the fund's performance does not perfectly match the index.
